Simple Process
Book a provider in 3 easy steps

No complicated forms or long waits. Find, book, and track your provider from your phone in minutes.

STEP 01
🔍

Browse & Discover

Search for the service you need or browse workers near you on a live map. Filter by distance, service type, and rating.

STEP 02
💬

Connect & Book

Unlock a chat with your chosen provider using 1 credit. Discuss job details, confirm availability, and send a booking request.

STEP 03
📍

Track in Real-Time

Once confirmed, track your provider live on the map. Get notified when they're nearby, almost there, and arrived.

STEP 04
⭐

Review & Rate

After the job is done, leave an honest review. Your feedback rewards excellent providers and helps the community.

Why Us
Built on trust, designed for ease

We don't just list workers — we build a platform where quality, safety, and convenience come standard.

🛡️

Verified Providers

Every provider goes through selfie verification and profile review before appearing on Fixora. You only see who we trust.

📍

Live GPS Tracking

Track your provider's real-time location on the map. Get automatic notifications as they get closer to you.

💰

Credits System

Get 5 free credits on sign-up. Use 1 credit to unlock a chat with any provider. Top up via GCash, Maya, or card.

💬

Direct Messaging

Chat directly with your provider before, during, and after your booking. Discuss scope, timing, and any special requests.

⭐

Honest Reviews

Reviews only come from verified completed bookings — no fake ratings. Every star is earned from a real job done.

Seamless App
